### Networking fundamentals

- IP Addressing: Understanding IPv4 and IPv6, Router, Switches, Route table, Gateway, Repeater, Hub, subnetting, and CIDR notation.
- OSI Model: Overview of the seven layers of the OSI model and their functions in network communication.
- Routing: Basics of routing protocols (e.g., OSPF, BGP) and how data packets are directed across networks.
- Switching: Concepts of Layer 2 switching, VLANs, and MAC address tables.
- Network Topologies: Different network layouts (e.g., star, mesh, bus) and their advantages and disadvantages.
- Network Security: Firewalls, VPNs, intrusion detection/prevention systems, and best practices for securing network infrastructure.
- Protocols: Common networking protocols (e.g., TCP/IP, HTTP, FTP, DNS) and their roles in communication.
- Wireless Networking: Wi-Fi standards, wireless security, and considerations for deploying wireless networks.
- Network Troubleshooting: Tools and techniques for diagnosing and resolving network issues, including ping, traceroute, and packet sniffing.
- Network Performance: Monitoring and optimizing network performance, including bandwidth management and Quality of Service (QoS).
